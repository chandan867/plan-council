# Pre-mortem: a year on, this did not work. Why?

Assume the plan was followed and the number barely moved. Work backwards to the most likely reasons. Look for:

- The assumption that everything else rests on and that nobody tested. Say how to test it this week, cheaply.
- A bet that takes months before any signal, with no earlier checkpoint.
- Dependence on something outside the team's control: a platform, an algorithm, a partner, a hire.
- The team stopping halfway because the plan needs more time, money or people than it admits.
- A competitor or market shift that makes the bet irrelevant.

Rank by likelihood times damage. Skip remote hypotheticals.

# Numbers: does the arithmetic reach the target?

Redo the math from the baseline to the goal. Look for:

- Baselines with no source, or that disagree with data in this directory.
- Expected effects with no reasoning behind them, or conversion and growth rates far above what this business has ever achieved.
- Effects added together when they overlap, or multiplied when they do not compound.
- Costs, time and capacity left out: who does the work, what it costs to acquire a customer versus what one is worth.
- A total that does not reach the stated target even if every bet works.

Show your calculation and the realistic number.

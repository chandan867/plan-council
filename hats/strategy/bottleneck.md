# Bottleneck: is this the constraint that actually limits the goal?

A plan that works on the wrong constraint achieves nothing. Look for:

- A diagnosis with no evidence, or evidence that points somewhere else. Check the numbers and notes in this directory yourself.
- Effort aimed at a step that is not the limiting one: more traffic when conversion is the problem, more features when nobody finds the product, more leads when delivery is full.
- A constraint the plan does not mention at all: cash, people, a platform dependency, a legal limit, the founder's own time.
- The plan treating a symptom as the cause.

Say what the real constraint is and what you saw that shows it.

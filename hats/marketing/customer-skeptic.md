# Customer skeptic: would the target person care?

Read the plan as the exact person it targets, busy and unconvinced. Look for:

- An audience so broad that the message fits nobody in particular.
- A problem stated in the company's words rather than the customer's.
- No reason to act now, or no reason to pick this over what they already use.
- A call to action that asks for more than the message has earned.
- The first objection this person would raise, left unanswered.

Say what this person would need to see or hear instead.

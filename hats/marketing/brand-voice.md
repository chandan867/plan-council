# Brand and voice: does it sound like a person at this company?

Look for:

- Generic filler that could be about any product. Quote it.
- Hype words, stacked adjectives and vague benefit claims in place of a concrete one.
- Tone or visual direction that contradicts the brand material or rules files in this directory.
- Copy that will not fit its format (title length, character limits, above-the-fold space).

For each, give the plainer or more specific version.

# Channel and evidence: is this the right place, and how do we know?

Look for:

- A channel chosen by habit, with no evidence the audience is there or that it worked before.
- Existing data in this directory (analytics exports, reports, past results) that the plan ignores or contradicts.
- Effort out of proportion to realistic reach.
- Missing distribution: the thing gets made, but nothing says how people will find it.
- Dependencies on accounts, approvals, budgets or lead times the plan does not mention.

If you have data tools available, use them and cite what you saw.

# Measurement: will we know whether it worked?

Look for:

- No single primary metric, or a metric that does not follow from the goal.
- No baseline, no check date, or no owner for the check.
- Several changes shipping together so that no result can be attributed to any of them.
- Tracking that the plan assumes exists. Check for it in this directory where you can.
- No stated result that would make the team stop or change course.

Suggest the smallest measurement setup that would settle the question.

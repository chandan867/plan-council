## How to combine

- Read every candidate to the end and score them against the rubric yourself before reading the verdict again. If you and the judge disagree on the base, one of you is biased or the rubric was unclear: read both reasons, decide, and say why.
- Start from the base. Take from the others only what is clearly better, and rewrite it so the plan still reads as one design. Do not paste sections together and do not average.
- Where all candidates agree, keep the shared shape. Where they contradict each other on a fact, check the directory.
- If a candidate saw a risk or an option the others missed, it belongs in the plan even if that candidate lost.

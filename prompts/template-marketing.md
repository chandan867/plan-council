1. **Goal.** The one number this should move, its current value if known, and by when.
2. **Audience.** Who exactly, what problem they have in their own words, and what they use today instead.
3. **Message and offer.** The main claim, the supporting points, and the call to action.
4. **Claims list.** Every factual claim about the product or the market, each with its source (a file in this directory, a data source, a public page). Mark claims with no source as unverified.
5. **Channels.** Where this runs and why there, with the evidence behind the choice.
6. **Deliverables and steps.** What gets made, in order, and which steps need a human sign-off before anything is generated or published.
7. **Measurement.** The metric, the baseline, the check date, and how you will tell this work caused the change.
8. **Assumptions and open questions.** What you did not verify, and decisions only the human can make.

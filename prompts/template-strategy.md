1. **Goal.** The number that must move, its value today, the target, and the date. State where today's value comes from.
2. **Where things stand.** The funnel or system as it is now, with real numbers from this directory at each step. Mark every number you estimated.
3. **The bottleneck.** The one constraint that most limits the goal today, with the evidence for it. Say what you ruled out.
4. **Options considered.** Two to four different bets, each with what must be true for it to work, and why the chosen ones win.
5. **The bets.** At most three, ranked. For each: what is done, the expected effect on the number with the arithmetic shown, the cost in time and money, and what it depends on.
6. **Does it add up?** Multiply the expected effects out. If the bets together do not reach the target, say so and say what the realistic number is.
7. **First 30 days.** The concrete steps, in order, with an owner for each. Start with the cheapest test of the riskiest assumption.
8. **Kill criteria.** For each bet, the result by a given date that means stop or change course.
9. **Not doing.** What is deliberately left out, so effort is not spread thin.
10. **Assumptions and open questions.** What you did not verify, and decisions only the human can make.
